§01 · the thesis

Work is becoming a workload. Workloads get operating systems.

Every generation of compute got an operating system the moment the workload outgrew hand management: batch jobs got schedulers, the desktop got windows, the cloud got orchestrators. Agentic work — software that does jobs, not just models that answer prompts — is the next workload, and today it is run by hand: unversioned prompts, unverified output, unmeasured spend, lessons lost when the session ends.

An operating system for work has five obligations: schedule the work, verify the result, remember what was learned, improve with use, and meter what everything cost. stella already meets all five for one seat. oxagen's platform is those same five obligations at the scale of an organization.

an operating system hasstella has — today, in the public tree
a kernelstella-core — the decision loop as pure functions over owned data. No I/O in the kernel is an enforced architectural invariant, not a style preference.
syscalls & driversports — a Provider trait for any model vendor, a ToolExecutor trait for any capability. Nine hosted providers plus any local server are adapters, never rewrites.
processessessions → turns → steps, every one journaled to a replayable event log. A run can be reconstructed after the fact, byte for byte.
a filesystemlocal-first SQLite state: executions and cost telemetry, durable memories, episodic context, a tree-sitter code graph.
memory protectionthe witness protocol — work is done when an independently authored test flips fail → pass. The worker cannot grade its own work.
a security modelzero telemetry egress by default. The exportable enterprise rollup is a reviewed, content-free allowlist enforced in CI — prompts, code, and paths are structurally unexportable.
a package managerskills the agent mines from its own reflections, and tools it builds for itself — each new tool admitted only after a capability witness proves it does something genuinely new.

The kernel is open — AGPL, bring-your-own-key, nothing phones home. The operating system around it is the business.

§02 · the engine, shipped

One deterministic loop, behind ports.

stella is a single-threaded engine: plan, fan tools out in parallel, observe, compact when noisy, repeat — with the budget consulted only at safe boundaries and every decision replayable from the event log. Everything vendor-shaped lives behind a port, which is why a new model provider or a new tool is an adapter, and why the engine that exists today can drive models that do not exist yet.

§03 · the laboratory

Improvement is an experiment, or it is an anecdote.

Most agent products improve by vibes: a prompt edit, a demo, a feeling. We run the other way. Every improvement to stella starts as a falsifiable hypothesis, bound to a feature branch, a pinned dataset, and a benchmark — preregistered before the first trial. Runs produce traces; the trace is ground truth; the conclusion comes from the trace or it is not drawn. That discipline is already written into this repository's contributor contract and practiced on stella itself — the paired run above shipped with its preregistration, its evidence directory, and a one-script reproduction.

A benchmark number you cannot reproduce is marketing. Ours reproduce from the evidence directory alone — and the same machinery will manage our customers' experiments, not just ours.

§04 · the flywheel, already turning

The engine improves itself — with receipts.

Self-improvement here is not a roadmap slide. The mechanisms are shipped features with tests and storage schemas behind them, reviewable in the open tree today. The agent's telemetry, state, memories, and histories are all first-class — journaled locally, loaded deterministically, measured for effect.

§05 · the model factory

Verified work labels itself. We turn it into your model.

Every witness flip yields a training example with ground truth attached at creation: the goal, the context, the diff, and a deterministic proof that it worked. No human labelers, no judge models, no scraping — the label is the flip. That makes a customer's own verified traces the highest-grade training corpus that exists for their work, and stella captures them as a side effect of doing the job.

Your traces stop training the lab you rent — they start training the model you own. You can leave with the weights; what compounds for oxagen is the machine that made them.

§06 · the economics

We sell no tokens and serve no GPUs.

The engine is free and bring-your-own-key: frontier model spend flows from the customer to their provider, never through us. Private models are bring-your-own-infrastructure: training and serving run on compute, network, and disk the customer already pays for. oxagen operates no inference fleet — so what we sell carries no per-token cost of goods underneath it.

community open source
the whole engine, free forever — AGPL, bring-your-own-key, zero egress. The wedge that earns bottom-up trust among engineers who choose their own tools.
pro support
paid technical support with a response SLA, upgrade guidance, and priority on the issue queue — people revenue, priced as such.
enterprise control plane
managed governance, content-free operational telemetry, the training pipeline, and metered usage credits on the private models it produces — margin revenue, by construction.

A frontier vendor's revenue is your consumption — every retry is their upside. Ours is the inverse: every token routing removes from your bill is our feature, not our lost revenue.

Each major version is the previous one's discipline, productized one level up: what we practice on ourselves in 1.x becomes what customers operate in 2.0; what 2.0 measures becomes what 3.0 compounds.
