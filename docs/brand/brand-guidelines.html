01 · The Idea

stella is Latin for star. The mark is a comet — a four-point star moving fast enough to leave a trail. It carries the whole positioning without a word of copy: the star is the benchmark result, the trail is the speed, and the direction is always forward.

One shape

Three trails and a star. Drawable from memory, legible at 16px, unmistakable at 6K.

One color

Phosphor Gold #FFB000 — the amber a CRT terminal glowed, and the gold star you get for being best in class. Everything else is ink, paper, and restraint.

Assemble, don't spin

stella motion never rotates or bounces idly. Elements arrive fast, settle precisely, and leave when done — like a well-run process.

Terminal-native

Monospace type, grid textures, block cursors. The brand looks like where the product lives.

02 · Logo

The logomark

The comet always travels left to right. Three trails — long, short, short — sit behind a four-point star. Proportions are fixed: star height 2×r, trail gap 1.55r, trail offsets ±0.64r, stroke 0.32r with round caps. Never redraw it; scale the provided files.

Clearspace: keep half a star-height (½✦) free on all sides. Nothing enters the box — no type, no edges, no other marks.

Color on ink · mono paper on ink · color on paper · mono ink on paper. Full-color gold works on both surfaces; mono is for one-color contexts (engraving, pinned tabs, embroidery).

Lockup & wordmark

The lockup pairs the comet with stella in JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, all lowercase, tracked −2%. The standalone wordmark is stella* — the footnote asterisk carries the claim (*faster, cheaper, more accurate) and doubles as the shell wildcard. Text is outlined in every file; no font required.

Always

  • lowercase, always — "stella", never "Stella" or "STELLA"
  • comet flies left→right; trails on the left
  • gold mark on ink or paper; mono where one color is forced
  • use lockup-adaptive.svg in docs — it switches with the user's color scheme
  • write the name as stella* in marketing copy when the footnote lands nearby

Never

  • rotate, flip, or make the comet fly right→left
  • recolor beyond gold / mono, add gradients, outlines, or shadows
  • stretch, add trails, or detach the star from its trails
  • set the wordmark in another typeface or with a capital S
  • place the mark on busy imagery without a glow or scrim
  • put gold body text on light surfaces (see contrast table)

04 · Typography

JetBrains Mono — the only family

The product lives in a terminal, so the brand speaks in monospace — everywhere, not just in code. JetBrains Mono is the voice: designed for reading code, tall x-height, generous counters, 0.6em advance, and it renders identically in the places stella runs. It is open source (SIL OFL 1.1) — the woff2 files ship in fonts/.

Fallback stack: 'JetBrains Mono', ui-monospace, 'SF Mono', Menlo, Consolas, 'Liberation Mono', monospace. Keep code ligatures on in product surfaces; the wordmark uses plain glyphs (no ligatures apply).

05 · Texture

Texture is what keeps big gold-on-ink surfaces from feeling empty — and it stays nearly subliminal. Four layers, three laws: keep every layer under 6% opacity (starfield highlights may reach 16%), never place texture behind body text, and never let texture touch the logomark's clearspace.

terminal grid — 96px cells, 1px lines at 5%, every 8th line 2×

starfield — 70% dots, 30% sparkles, seeded random, clears the mark

gold glow — radial, 10–13%, only ever behind the star

grain — fractal noise at 4–5%, banners and avatars only

06 · Motion

Assemble, don't spin. The spinners build the brand instead of rotating an arbitrary shape: trails streak in, the star lands with a small overshoot, and — when the work ships — the comet flies off right.

08 · Assets & Code

Using the CSS

css/globals.css is a shadcn/ui base file for Tailwind v4 — import it as your app's global stylesheet; light theme on :root, dark under .dark, all values in oklch, Phosphor Gold as --primary and --ring. css/tokens.css is framework-free brand tokens (ramps, type, motion, texture budgets) — safe to ship alongside any stack; use [data-theme] or .dark to switch, with system fallback built in.
