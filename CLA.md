## What this is, in plain language

- **You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment. You do not
  transfer ownership of anything, and you remain free to use your own
  contribution however you like, anywhere else, forever.
- **Your work stays open.** Every contribution is published under the

- **Oxagen may also include your contribution in commercially licensed builds.**
  This is the part that matters. Stella is [dual-licensed](LICENSING.md): AGPL
  for everyone, commercial licences for those who cannot accept the AGPL's
  terms. Without a sublicensing grant, a contribution would be AGPL-only, and
  could never be shipped in a commercial build — one merged pull request would
  permanently remove a file from the commercial track. Commercial licensing is
  what funds the work.
- **You confirm it is yours to give.** You wrote it, or you have the right to
  contribute it.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot will comment on it with the exact sentence to
reply with — a single comment, from the account that authored the commits:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your signature is recorded, with your GitHub username and the commit SHA, in
[`macanderson/stella-cla-signatures`](https://github.com/macanderson/stella-cla-signatures).
You sign once; every later pull request from the same account is covered
automatically.

**Contributing on behalf of your employer?** Make sure you have their permission
(Section 4.3). If your employer requires a signed corporate agreement, email
<licensing@oxagen.sh> and we will send one.

Questions about any of this are welcome — open a discussion or email
<licensing@oxagen.sh>. If some clause is a blocker for you, say so; it is better
to talk about it than to have you walk away.
